Introduction to Artificial Intelligence

Artificial Intelligence (AI) refers to the simulation of human intelligence in machines that are programmed to think like humans and mimic their actions. It focuses on learning, reasoning and self-correction.


Core Fields of AI

1- Popular Applications

  • Virtual Assistants (Siri, Alexa)
  • Recommendation Engines (Netflix)
  • Autonomous Vehicles
  • Facial Recognition

2- Main Technologies

  • Machine Learning - Systems that learn from data
  • Deep Learning - Nearul networks modeling
  • Natural Language Processing (NLP) - Text and Speech analysis

3- Types of AI

  1. Artificial Narrow Intelligence (ANI)
  2. Artificial General Intelligence (AGI)
  3. Artificial Super Intelligence (ASI)
  4. Generative AI (e.g, ChatGPT)
